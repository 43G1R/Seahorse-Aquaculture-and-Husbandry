Seahorse Aquaculture and Husbandry

Protecting and Understanding Philippine Seahorses

Seahorses are among the most unique marine animals found in Philippine waters. They play an important role in maintaining healthy coastal ecosystems but face threats from habitat destruction, pollution, and overfishing.

This website presents our thesis on Seahorse Husbandry, which focuses on creating a suitable environment for raising and caring for seahorses while promoting conservation and sustainable practices.

Our Objectives:

  • Study the proper care and husbandry of seahorses.
  • Design an emulated habitat that supports their health and well-being.
  • Promote awareness about seahorse conservation.
  • Contribute knowledge that may help future breeding and preservation efforts.

Why Seahorse Husbandry Matters

Proper husbandry techniques can improve the survival and welfare of seahorses in captivity while reducing pressure on wild populations. Through research and responsible care, we can help conserve these remarkable marine creatures for future generations.
